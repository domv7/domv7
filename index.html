DominikNarnhofer_

I’m a researcher at ETH Zurich, working on computer vision and machine learning in the PRS group. My research interests include generative models, 3D computer vision, and fundamental machine learning.

I received my PhD from TU Graz in 2023 under the supervision of Thomas Pock with the thesis, Generative Models and Uncertainty for Inverse Problems in Imaging.
